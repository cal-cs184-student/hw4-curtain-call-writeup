Part 1: Masses and Springs

I built an evenly-spaced grid of point masses and springs using two sets of double for loops, with the first creating all point masses and setting their locations appropriately and the second creating springs between the masses. The springs represent structural, shearing, or bending constraints, and are only applied between masses at specific positions relative to one another.
